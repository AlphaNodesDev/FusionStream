# FusionStream Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

FusionStream is published by SCIFY TECHNOLOGIES PRIVATE LIMITED ("we"). This policy explains what
the FusionStream desktop app does with your information.

## What we collect

Nothing. FusionStream has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no telemetry. We do not
run servers that receive your data.

## Data stored on your device

The app stores the following only on your computer, so it can work:

- Your library (saved titles), watch progress and settings
- The list of addons you installed
- Temporary video cache while you watch (deleted when the app closes)

You can clear watch history and reset addons in Settings. Uninstalling the app removes all of it.

## Connections to third parties

To show catalogs, information, subtitles and videos, the app connects directly from your computer
to the services you use, including:

- Cinemeta (strem.io) for movie and series information and artwork
- OpenSubtitles (strem.io) for subtitles
- Internet Archive (archive.org) for public-domain films
- Any other addon you choose to install

These services receive your IP address and the requests needed to load content, like any website
you visit. Their own privacy policies apply. We do not control third-party addons.

## Children

FusionStream is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from them.

## Changes

We will update this page if the app's behaviour changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
